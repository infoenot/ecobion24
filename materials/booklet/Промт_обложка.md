# Промт для обложки евробуклета (ChatGPT)

Перед промтом загрузить в чат `materials/Оконщик_3000_технология_для_ChatGPT.md` со словами «Изучи технологию. Изображение должно ей соответствовать».

Размер: **вертикальный 1024×1536 (2:3), максимальное качество**. Текста на картинке нет — заголовок, волны и плашку накладываем при вёрстке.

```
Photorealistic vertical photo, shot on iPhone 15 Pro, natural warm golden-hour light, 2:3 portrait.
A cozy glazed loggia in a modern Moscow new-build apartment tower, 18th floor, view of the city and green trees at sunset.
Floor-to-ceiling aluminium glazing with slim dark frames. EVERY vertical mullion and the low horizontal transom are covered with slim seamless matte WHITE thermal boxes (U-shaped PVC casings 120 mm wide, crisp 90° edges, no visible joints or screws), running straight from floor to ceiling. Glass size unchanged.
Interior: light oak laminate floor, a woven jute rug, one cream boucle armchair with a knitted throw, a small round oak side table with a ceramic coffee cup and a book, an olive tree in a beige pot. Calm, airy, warm beige and white palette with soft terracotta accents (one terracotta cushion).
Camera at eye level, slight angle along the loggia so the row of white mullion boxes leads the eye into depth; boxes clearly visible and sharp in the foreground.
Composition for a print cover: keep the TOP 25% of the frame simple and light (bright sky / soft ceiling, no busy details) for a headline; keep the BOTTOM 20% calm (floor and rug) for a colour band. Main subject in the middle.
No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people.
No LED strips, no glowing lights in or along the boxes, no wooden slat walls, no curved or bulky casings, no glossy plastic look, no second row of glazing.
```

Если картинка удачная, но мелкая — попросить «upscale to maximum resolution, keep every detail».
